Why SmartNotes?

SmartNotes is built on the principle that your personal knowledge should remain personal. We combine cutting-edge AI technology with local-first architecture to give you powerful note-taking without compromising privacy.

Unlike cloud-based alternatives, SmartNotes processes everything on your device. Your notes, your embeddings, your knowledge graph - all stored locally and encrypted.

Built by the AOSSIE community with ❤️ for students, researchers, writers, and anyone who values both intelligence and privacy.
